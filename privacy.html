Inkling — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 14, 2026

Inkling is a personal calendar, notes, and reminders app with an assistant called Inkling. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep this deliberately short and plain.

The short version

  • We collect only what's needed to run your account and save your calendar.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data, and we don't show ads.
  • You can use Inkling without an account ("Continue without signing in") — then your data stays on your device only.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details — if you create an account: your email address, an optional username, and your password. Your password is stored only as a salted cryptographic hash; we never store or see it in plain text.
  • Your content — the calendar events, notes, appointments, reminders/alerts, goals, and notification preferences you create in the app. This is saved to your account so it syncs across your devices and survives restarts.
  • Basic usage analytics — we use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to count page visits. It does not use cookies, does not track you across sites, and does not collect personal information — only aggregate visit counts.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, or device identifiers.

Guest mode

If you choose "Continue without signing in," Inkling runs entirely on your device. Your notes and calendar are stored in your browser's local storage and are never sent to our server. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling removes them.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To save and sync your calendar and notes across your devices.
  • To deliver the reminders and notifications you set up.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how many people use Inkling so we can improve it.

Where your data is stored

The app is served over HTTPS from GitHub Pages. Account data is processed by our backend (hosted on Render) and stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (Neon). These providers host data on our behalf and do not use it for their own purposes. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS).

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data with the infrastructure providers above that are needed to run the service, and when required by law.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account data until you ask us to delete it. To delete your account and all associated data, email us at inkling.app0@gmail.com from the address on your account, and we'll remove it. Guest-mode data isn't held by us — you delete it yourself by clearing your browser data.

Security

Passwords are hashed, connections are encrypted with TLS, and sessions use signed tokens. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children

Inkling is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update this page and revise the effective date above.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email inkling.app0@gmail.com.
